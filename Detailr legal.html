Privacy Policy

What data this app collects

None, on any server. There is no account, no login, and no sign-up — nothing about you is ever sent anywhere. Every job you create (client names, phone numbers, vehicle details, prices, notes, and before/after photos) is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Camera & photo library

Camera and photo library access is used only to attach before/after photos to a job. Those photos stay on your device unless you explicitly use the Share feature to send a summary or a photo yourself — that share happens through your device's own share sheet, to whatever app or contact you choose.

Your clients' information

The names, phone numbers, addresses, and vehicle photos you enter for a job belong to your clients, not to us — we never see them, since nothing leaves your device. You're the one who decides to store that information, so you're responsible for handling it appropriately: get whatever consent is expected where you do business before saving a client's contact details or photographing their car, and honor a request to remove someone's information (Job Detail → Delete Job permanently removes a client's data from the app, including their photos).

Notifications

Job reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled entirely on your device. No reminder or job data is sent to any server to make this work.

How your data is protected

There's no server for anyone to break into — job records and photos exist only in the app's private, sandboxed storage on your phone, behind the same passcode, fingerprint, or face unlock you already use to unlock your device. The app also offers its own optional App Lock (Settings → Security) as a second layer. If you have iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Android's Auto Backup turned on, that backup inherits your phone's own platform backup encryption; the app doesn't add a separate password or encryption layer of its own, and doesn't operate any servers that could be breached, because none exist.

Analytics, tracking & ads

None. The app has no analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no third-party SDKs of any kind, and doesn't track your usage. Nothing about how you use the app is ever collected, measured, or shared with anyone.

Backups & deleting your data

If you have iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Auto Backup for Apps (Android) turned on for your phone — both standard, on-by-default features of their platform — the app's local data is included as part of your regular device backup. That's Apple's or Google's normal behavior for apps, not something the app does on its own; it also means reinstalling the app on the same account can restore old data rather than starting empty. You can also make your own backup any time from Settings → Export / Backup Data.

To delete everything, use Settings → Clear All Data, or simply delete the app from your phone — since nothing is stored anywhere else, either one immediately and permanently removes every job record and photo (on Android, a plain uninstall may still be restored by Auto Backup on reinstall; clearing the app's storage first, or turning off backup for the app, avoids that).

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Because nothing about your business ever reaches a server, most formal data-rights requests — access, correction, export, deletion — are already satisfied by design: there's nowhere else your data exists for us to act on. You already have complete access and control, right now, from Settings: Export / Backup Data gives you a full copy of everything at any time, and Clear All Data (or simply deleting the app) removes everything, immediately and permanently. We hold nothing about you or your clients to report, correct, or hand over, because we never receive it in the first place.

Children's privacy

This app is a business tool meant for adults running a detailing business. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information about children beyond whatever a client happens to mention in a job's notes.

Questions about this policy

Reach out any time via the app's Settings → Send Feedback.
